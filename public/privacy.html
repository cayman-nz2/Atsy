Privacy

What we keep, and for how long

A CV is one of the densest personal documents you own. This page says exactly what Atsy holds, who can read it, and how to remove it. No legal wall, no hedging.

What Atsy stores today

  • Your email address — so a sign-in code can reach you and you can come back to your results. Kept until you delete your account.
  • The country your request came from — used only to count where sign-ups come from.
  • Sign-in codes, stored as a one-way hash, deleted the moment they are used and expiring after ten minutes either way.
  • A salted hash of your IP address — never the address itself — purely to stop someone flooding the sign-in form.

Nothing on this page is aspirational. It describes what happens to a CV you upload today.

Your CV, once you upload one

  • Your file is encrypted before it is stored, with a key held by the application, not by the storage. A leaked storage bucket yields ciphertext.
  • The file is deleted automatically within 24 hours; what Atsy read about its layout goes after 30 days. Both happen on a schedule, not on request.
  • The text of your CV is never stored as text. The full extraction — your name, your email, your employers, your bullet points — lives only for the seconds it takes to score, goes to the browser that uploaded it, and is never written down. What is kept is facts about the document: how many columns, which sections were found, whether the dates line up. The one exception is the short quotes in the next point, which are the evidence under each finding.
  • The name you gave the file is kept with the scan record for 30 days, so you can tell your scans apart in your history. CV filenames very often contain your own name — priya-raman-cv.pdf — so if you would rather that were not stored, rename the file before you upload it. The file itself is stored under a random id, never under its name.
  • Findings are kept with short quotes capped at 120 characters, so a fix can point at what it refers to, and with the rectangle on the page the finding is about — four numbers, no text. That is what lets the X-ray mark your own page.
  • The X-ray draws your PDF in your browser, not on a server. The file is the same one the “open the stored copy” button gives you, decrypted for you and nobody else, and it stops being available when the file is deleted at 24 hours.
  • Nobody at Atsy can read your CV. There is no admin screen for it; the only view is yours, and a test in the build fails if that ever changes.
  • If you ask for AI-written suggestions, your name, email, phone, links and employers are removed before anything is sent, one bullet at a time. Scoring itself never uses AI.
  • Delete everything in your account, any time, immediately.

Where your data physically sits

Atsy runs entirely on Cloudflare. There is no second host, no backup service and no analytics platform — the list below is the whole of it.

  • Your account row, your scan records and their findings are in a Cloudflare D1 database called atsy-db-oc.
  • Your encrypted CV is in a Cloudflare R2 bucket called atsy-cv-oc, stored under a random id with no filename and no user id attached to the object.
  • Both sit in Cloudflare's Oceania region, and D1 reports serving the database from Auckland. Read replication is switched off, so there is no second copy of the database anywhere else.
  • Being honest about what that guarantees. Cloudflare lets us ask for a region, and it is honouring that ask — but a region is a placement request, not a contractual boundary. The only hard boundaries Cloudflare sells are the EU and a US government one; there is no New Zealand or Australian equivalent to buy. So: your data is in Oceania, and Atsy cannot promise it can never be moved.
  • If you are in the UK or the EU, your data is still transferred out of the UK or EU to be stored — to Oceania rather than to North America, but a transfer under the GDPR either way, and worth knowing before you upload.
  • The code itself runs at Cloudflare's edge, in whichever data centre is nearest you. Your upload travels from you to that edge and is encrypted there, inside the application, before it is written to storage in Oceania.
  • Sign-in codes are sent through Cloudflare Email Service. Once the message leaves, it is with your own mail provider, which Atsy does not control.
  • If you ask for an AI rewrite, the redacted bullet goes to Cloudflare Workers AI. Atsy does not pin a region for that one, so it is processed wherever Cloudflare runs the model. It carries no name, email, phone, link or employer.
  • The copy of each Atsy email that goes to the person who runs it lands in an ordinary hosted mailbox, which is outside the Cloudflare storage described above.

These two regions are read back from Cloudflare on every deploy and checked against this page. If either ever moved and this paragraph were not updated, the deploy would fail rather than leave you reading something untrue. Atsy previously stored both in eastern North America — not by choice, but because the script that created them never said where, so they were placed next to the machine that ran it.

What Atsy never does

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts — the only outside code on the whole site is Cloudflare's bot check on the sign-in and upload forms.
  • Your CV is never sold, shared, published, or used to train anything.
  • No recruiter, employer or agency has access to any of this.
  • No email from us except your sign-in codes and answers to feedback you send.
  • A copy of every email Atsy sends you also goes to the person who runs it, so that support questions can be answered and problems spotted. Sign-in codes are masked in that copy — it can never be used to get into your account.

Your rights, and how to use them

Atsy is run from New Zealand under the Privacy Act 2020, and follows the GDPR standard for anyone in the UK or EU — including for the transfer out of the UK or EU described above. You can see everything held about you (the app is that view), correct it by signing in again with the right address, take it with you by downloading your report, and erase it in one click from your account screen. Erasure is immediate and irreversible.

Questions, or something you think is wrong here: reply to any Atsy email and it reaches a person.
